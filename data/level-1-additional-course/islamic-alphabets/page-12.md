# Kabah: The House of Allah

## What is the Kabah

The Kabah is the sacred house of Allah located in Makkah, Saudi Arabia. It is the qiblah that we all face during prayer from anywhere in the world.

## Building of the Kabah

The Kabah was first built by Prophet Ibrahim and his son Ismail upon Allah's command. It has been rebuilt several times throughout history and remains our most sacred site.

## Significance of the Kabah

The Kabah is the center of our worship. We face its direction five times a day in prayer, symbolizing our unity and devotion to the One God.

## The Black Stone

The Black Stone is set in the eastern corner of the Kabah. It was given to Ibrahim by Jibreel and we kiss or point to it during Tawaf as a sunnah of our Prophet.

## Kiswah: The Covering

The Kabah is covered with a black cloth called Kiswah, embroidered with gold Quranic verses. It is replaced annually during Hajj.

## Tawaf Around the Kabah

We circle the Kabah seven times in Tawaf during Umrah and Hajj. This act shows our complete submission and love for Allah, circling His house in devotion.

## Makkah: The Sacred City

Makkah is our holiest city where the Kabah is located. Non-Muslims are not permitted to enter its boundaries, preserving its sanctity.

## Virtues of Praying at the Kabah

Praying in Masjid al-Haram carries immense reward — one prayer there is equal to 100,000 prayers elsewhere.

## Common Mistakes

Some people think we worship the Kabah or treat it as an idol. We worship only Allah; the Kabah is simply our direction of prayer and a sacred symbol.
