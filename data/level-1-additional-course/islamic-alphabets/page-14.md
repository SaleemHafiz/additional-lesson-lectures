# Mosque: The House of Allah

## Meaning of Mosque

A mosque is a place where we gather to worship Allah, especially for the five daily prayers. It is the most beloved place on earth to Allah.

## Importance of the Mosque

The mosque is the center of our community life. It is not only for prayer but also for learning, teaching, social gatherings, and strengthening brotherhood among us.

## Etiquette of Entering

We should enter the mosque with the right foot, saying the specific dua for entering. We should be clean, dressed modestly, and show respect and calmness inside.

## Etiquette of Leaving

We should leave the mosque with the left foot, saying the specific dua for leaving. We should maintain respect even after leaving.

## Praying in Congregation

Praying in congregation at the mosque is highly emphasized, especially for men. The reward for congregational prayer is 27 times greater than praying alone.

## The First Mosque

The first mosque in our history was the Quba Mosque built by the Prophet near Madinah. He also established Masjid al-Nabawi as the center of our community.

## The Three Sacred Mosques

The three most sacred mosques are Masjid al-Haram, Masjid al-Nabawi, and Masjid al-Aqsa. Prayer in these mosques carries special rewards.

## Children in the Mosque

The Prophet allowed children in the mosque and taught them gently. We should bring our children to the mosque to teach them love for the house of Allah.

## Common Mistakes

Some among us talk loudly, use phones, or engage in worldly conversations inside the mosque. Others neglect praying at the mosque regularly without a valid excuse.
