# Neighbour: Rights and Responsibilities

## Importance of Neighbours

Our faith places great emphasis on the rights of neighbours. The Prophet said that Jibreel repeatedly advised him about neighbours until he thought neighbours would inherit from one another.

## Rights of Neighbours

Neighbours have the right to our kindness, help in need, and protection from harm. We must share food with neighbours, visit them when sick, and avoid causing them trouble.

## Kindness to Neighbours

The Prophet said that whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day should be kind to their neighbour. This includes greeting them, helping them, and being patient with them.

## Neighbours in Difficulty

If a neighbour is hungry, sick, or in need, we must help them regardless of their religion. Our true faith is shown by caring for those who live nearby.

## Avoiding Harm to Neighbours

We should not harm our neighbour by loud noise, blocking their path, or any other form of disturbance. The Prophet said such a person does not have true faith.

## Patience with Neighbours

Sometimes neighbours may have faults or cause inconvenience. Our faith teaches us patience, forgiveness, and dealing with them in the best possible manner.

## Neighbours of Different Faiths

We are taught to treat neighbours well regardless of their religion. The Prophet treated his Jewish neighbours with kindness and visited them when they were sick.

## Teaching Children

We should teach our children to respect neighbours, share with them, and not disturb them. This builds a strong and caring community from a young age.

## Common Mistakes

Some among us ignore their neighbours completely or harm them through noise or misbehaviour. Others forget that neighbours have rights even if they are not relatives or friends.
