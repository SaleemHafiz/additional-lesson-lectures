# Prophet: The Chosen Messengers of Allah

## Meaning of Prophet

A prophet is a man chosen by Allah to receive revelation and guide people to the truth. Prophets are the best of humanity, sinless in delivering the message, and perfect examples for their nations.

## Nabi and Rasool

A Nabi receives revelation to guide his people, while a Rasool is given a new scripture or law. Every messenger is a prophet, but not every prophet is a messenger.

## Purpose of Prophets

All prophets were sent with the same core message: worship Allah alone and avoid false gods. They also taught us good morals, warned against evil, and gave glad tidings of Paradise.

## First and Last Prophet

The first prophet was Adam, and the final prophet is Muhammad. No prophet will come after him, as his message is complete and universal for all humanity.

## Prophet Muhammad

Muhammad is our final messenger sent as mercy for all worlds. He received the Quran, established our complete religion, and lived as the perfect model for humanity.

## Prophet Ibrahim

Ibrahim is known as the friend of Allah and the father of monotheism. He broke the idols of his people, passed great tests from Allah, and built the Kabah with his son Ismail.

## Prophet Musa

Musa was sent to the Children of Israel with the Torah. He faced Pharaoh with miracles, led his people out of Egypt, and taught them the laws of Allah.

## Prophet Isa

Isa was born miraculously without a father and performed miracles by Allah's permission. He preached Tawheed, and we believe he was not killed but raised to heaven.

## Belief in All Prophets

We must believe in all prophets without rejecting any. Rejecting one prophet is like rejecting all, as all were sent by the same Allah with the same message of Tawheed.
