# Quran: The Final Revelation

## What is the Quran

The Quran is the final divine book revealed by Allah to our Prophet Muhammad through Angel Jibreel. It is the speech of Allah, preserved in Arabic, and our primary source of guidance.

## Revelation of the Quran

The Quran was revealed over 23 years in stages. It came gradually to guide our community through different situations and to make learning and implementation easier for us.

## Structure of the Quran

The Quran contains 114 surahs and over 6,000 ayahs. It is divided into 30 juz for easy recitation, especially during Ramadan.

## Language and Preservation

The Quran was revealed in clear Arabic. Allah has promised to preserve it, and it remains unchanged since revelation. It is memorized by millions of us and recited daily worldwide.

## Purpose of the Quran

The Quran guides us to worship Allah alone, live righteously, and prepare for the Hereafter. It provides us with laws, stories, warnings, and glad tidings for those who believe.

## Reading and Reciting

Reciting the Quran is an act of worship. Every letter we recite brings reward, and our Prophet said the best among us are those who learn and teach the Quran.

## Understanding the Quran

We should not only recite but also understand the Quran's meaning. Reflecting on its verses increases our faith and helps us apply its teachings in daily life.

## The Quran as Healing

The Quran is a source of healing for our physical and spiritual diseases. It brings peace to our hearts, removes anxiety, and guides those who seek the truth.

## Common Mistakes

Some among us recite the Quran without understanding or reflecting. Others treat it as a book of blessings only for the dead while ignoring its guidance for the living.
