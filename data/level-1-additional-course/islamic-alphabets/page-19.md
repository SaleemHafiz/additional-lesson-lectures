# Ramadan: The Month of Blessings

## Meaning of Ramadan

Ramadan is the ninth month of the Islamic calendar and our holiest month. It is the month when the Quran was first revealed and when fasting is obligatory for us.

## Fasting in Ramadan

We fast from dawn to sunset, abstaining from food, drink, and marital relations. Fasting is the fourth pillar of our faith and was prescribed in the second year of Hijrah.

## Purpose of Fasting

Fasting teaches us self-control, patience, and empathy for the hungry. It purifies our souls, strengthens our faith, and brings us closer to Allah.

## Tarawih Prayers

During Ramadan, we offer special night prayers called Tarawih in congregation at the mosque. These prayers include recitation of long portions of the Quran.

## Quran in Ramadan

Ramadan is the month of the Quran. We strive to recite and complete the entire Quran during this month, as Jibreel would review it with our Prophet each Ramadan.

## Laylatul Qadr

Laylatul Qadr is better than a thousand months. It falls in the last ten nights of Ramadan, and worship on this night brings us immense reward.

## Zakat al-Fitr

Before the end of Ramadan, we give Zakat al-Fitr, a small charity that purifies our fast and helps the poor celebrate Eid. It is obligatory on every one of us who can afford it.

## I'tikaf

I'tikaf is seclusion in the mosque during the last ten days of Ramadan. It is a sunnah for men and women to dedicate ourselves entirely to worship and prayer.

## Common Mistakes

Some among us fast from food but indulge in gossip, anger, and bad behavior. Others overeat at iftar and waste food, missing the spiritual purpose of Ramadan.
