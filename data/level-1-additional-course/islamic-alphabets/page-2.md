# Allah: The One True God

## Who is Allah

Allah is our Creator, Sustainer, and the Lord of everything that exists. He is the only One worthy of worship, with no partner, no equal, and no offspring.

## The Meaning of Allah

Allah is the personal name of the One True God in our faith. It combines all meanings of divinity, lordship, and perfect attributes.

## Tawheed: The Oneness of Allah

Tawheed means believing in Allah as One in His lordship, worship, and names and attributes. This is the foundation of our faith and the message of all prophets.

## Allah as Creator

Allah created the heavens, the earth, and everything between them without any help. He alone brings life from nothing and controls all affairs.

## Allah as Sustainer

Allah provides for all creatures, giving them food, water, air, and everything needed for survival. No one can provide except Him.

## Allah as All-Knowing

Allah knows everything—what is hidden and what is open, what is in the hearts and what will happen in the future. His knowledge covers all things.

## Allah as Most Merciful

Allah's mercy encompasses everything. He forgives sins, accepts repentance, and His mercy is greater than His anger.

## Our Relationship with Allah

We are created to worship Allah alone, obey His commands, and seek His pleasure. He is near to us, hears our prayers, and responds when we call.

## Common Mistakes

Some among us associate partners with Allah in worship or attributes. Others forget Allah in daily life and only remember Him in difficulty.
