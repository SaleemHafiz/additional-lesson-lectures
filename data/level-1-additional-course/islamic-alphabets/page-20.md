# Salah: The Pillar of Islam

## Meaning of Salah

Salah is our prayer performed five times daily. It is the second pillar of our faith and the most important act of worship after faith itself.

## The Five Daily Prayers

Our five prayers are Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha. Each prayer has a fixed number of units and is offered at specific times.

## Importance of Salah

Salah is the first matter we will be asked about on the Day of Judgment. If it is good, all our other deeds will be good. The Prophet called it the pillar of our religion.

## Conditions of Salah

Before praying, we must be in a state of purity through wudu, wear clean clothes, face the qiblah, and have the correct intention.

## Components of Salah

Salah includes standing, bowing, prostrating, sitting, and reciting verses from the Quran. Prostration is our highest state of humility before Allah.

## Salah in Congregation

Praying in congregation at the mosque is highly recommended for men. The reward for congregational prayer is 27 times greater than praying alone.

## Benefits of Salah

Salah prevents immorality and evil deeds, brings peace to our hearts, and strengthens our connection with Allah. It is our source of spiritual nourishment.

## Salah for Children

We should teach our children to pray from age seven and gently encourage them by age ten. Early training builds a lifelong habit of prayer.

## Common Mistakes

Some among us rush through prayer without concentration or understanding. Others delay or miss prayers due to laziness, forgetting that salah is our most important daily duty.
