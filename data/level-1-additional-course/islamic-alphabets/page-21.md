# Tawheed: The Oneness of Allah

## Meaning of Tawheed

Tawheed is our belief in the oneness of Allah in His lordship, worship, and names and attributes. It is the most fundamental concept in our faith and the message of all prophets.

## Tawheed ar-Rububiyyah

This is our belief that Allah alone is the Creator, Sustainer, and Controller of everything. He gives life and death, provides our sustenance, and manages all affairs.

## Tawheed al-Uluhiyyah

This is our belief that Allah alone deserves to be worshiped. All acts of worship — prayer, dua, sacrifice, hope, fear, love, and trust — must be directed only to Allah.

## Tawheed al-Asma was-Sifat

This is our belief in Allah's names and attributes as revealed in the Quran and Sunnah. We affirm them without distorting, denying, asking how, or making comparisons.

## Shirk: The Opposite

Shirk is associating partners with Allah in worship or attributes. It is the greatest sin in our faith, and Allah forgives all sins except shirk if a person dies without repentance.

## Major Shirk

Major shirk includes praying to other beings, sacrificing to other than Allah, or believing that anyone shares Allah's power. This takes a person out of our faith.

## Minor Shirk

Minor shirk includes showing off in worship or swearing by other than Allah. While not removing a person from faith, it diminishes the sincerity of our worship.

## Benefits of Tawheed

Tawheed brings us peace, clarity, and purpose in life. It frees our hearts from fear of creation, builds our reliance on Allah alone, and guarantees Paradise for those who die upon it.

## Common Mistakes

Some among us commit shirk without realizing it, such as wearing amulets for protection or believing in lucky charms. Others neglect learning Tawheed and its requirements.
