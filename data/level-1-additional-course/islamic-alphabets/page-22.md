# Umrah: The Lesser Pilgrimage

## Meaning of Umrah

Umrah is a pilgrimage to Makkah that we can perform at any time of the year. Unlike Hajj, it has no fixed date and consists of fewer rituals, but it carries great reward.

## Virtues of Umrah

Our Prophet said that performing Umrah expiates sins between it and the next Umrah. He also said that Umrah in Ramadan is equivalent to performing Hajj with him.

## Rituals of Umrah

Umrah consists of four main acts: entering Ihram, performing Tawaf around the Kabah, walking between Safa and Marwah, and shaving or trimming the hair.

## Ihram for Umrah

Before entering Makkah, we enter Ihram by making intention and wearing the prescribed garments. Men wear two white sheets, while women wear modest clothing.

## Tawaf in Umrah

We circle the Kabah seven times, starting from the Black Stone. We recite duas and remember Allah during each round, feeling the unity of believers from around the world.

## Sa'ee in Umrah

After Tawaf, we walk seven times between Safa and Marwah. This commemorates Hajar's desperate search for water for her son Ismail, teaching us to trust in Allah's mercy.

## Shaving or Trimming

After completing Sa'ee, men shave their heads or trim their hair, and women cut a small portion. This symbolizes our completion and humility before Allah.

## Benefits of Umrah

Umrah cleanses our souls, renews our faith, and brings us closer to Allah. It is an opportunity to visit the sacred city of Makkah and pray in Masjid al-Haram.

## Common Mistakes

Some among us delay Umrah thinking it is less important or only for the wealthy. Others perform the rituals without understanding their meaning or spiritual significance.
