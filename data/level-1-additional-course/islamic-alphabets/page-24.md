# Wudu: Purification Before Prayer

## Meaning of Wudu

Wudu is the act of washing specific body parts with water before prayer. It is a condition for the validity of our salah and a means of physical and spiritual purification.

## The Obligation

Allah commands in the Quran to perform wudu before prayer. Our Prophet said that the key to Paradise is prayer, and the key to prayer is purification.

## Steps of Wudu

Wudu involves washing the hands, rinsing the mouth, cleaning the nose, washing the face, washing the arms to the elbows, wiping the head and ears, and washing the feet to the ankles.

## Sunnah of Wudu

The Prophet taught us additional sunnah actions in wudu, such as using miswak, saying Bismillah at the start, rinsing the mouth and nose three times, and running fingers through the beard.

## Wudu as Worship

Wudu is not just physical cleaning but a spiritual act that prepares our hearts for standing before Allah. Each drop of water we use cleanses sins from our body parts.

## When Wudu is Required

Wudu becomes invalid after using the bathroom, passing wind, sleeping deeply, or losing consciousness. We must renew it before the next prayer.

## Nullifiers

Other nullifiers include bleeding from a wound, vomiting, and touching private parts directly. We should learn these rules to maintain valid wudu.

## Wudu Before Sleep

The Prophet recommended performing wudu before sleeping. It is a sunnah that brings us protection and blessings throughout the night.

## Common Mistakes

Some among us waste water excessively during wudu, which is against the Prophet's teaching. Others rush through the steps without proper washing or understanding the spiritual significance.
