# Wrong Deeds: Sins to Avoid

## Meaning of Wrong Deeds

Wrong deeds are actions that displease Allah and go against His commands. Our faith teaches us to avoid sins and seek forgiveness when we fall into error.

## Major Sins

Major sins include shirk, murder, theft, lying, consuming interest, and slandering innocent people. These require our sincere repentance and change.

## Minor Sins

Minor sins are smaller offences that we may commit daily. While less severe, we should not take them lightly, as persistent minor sins can harden our hearts.

## Sins of the Tongue

Gossip, backbiting, lying, slander, and harsh speech are serious sins. Our Prophet said that a Muslim is one from whose tongue and hands others are safe.

## Sins of the Heart

Arrogance, jealousy, hypocrisy, and malice are sins of the heart. These inner diseases destroy our good deeds and harm our relationships with others and with Allah.

## Sins of the Eyes and Ears

Looking at prohibited things and listening to harmful speech or music are sins. We must guard our senses as the Quran commands us to lower our gaze.

## Repentance from Sins

Allah accepts repentance from those who sincerely turn back to Him. True repentance means stopping the sin, regretting it, resolving not to return, and making amends if possible.

## Allah's Mercy

Allah's mercy is vast and covers all our sins for those who repent before death. Our Prophet said that the one who repents from sin is like one who never committed it.

## Common Mistakes

Some among us commit sins openly without shame or minimize their seriousness. Others despair of Allah's mercy, not realizing that Allah forgives all sins except dying upon shirk.
