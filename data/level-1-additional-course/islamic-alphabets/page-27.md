# Zakat: Purifying Wealth

## Meaning of Zakat

Zakat is an obligatory charity and the third pillar of our faith. It means purifying our wealth by giving a fixed portion to those in need, as commanded by Allah.

## Who Must Pay Zakat

Zakat is obligatory on every adult among us who owns wealth above a certain minimum for one lunar year. It applies to gold, silver, cash, business goods, and agricultural produce.

## The Amount of Zakat

The standard rate of Zakat is 2.5% of our savings and wealth held for one year. This small percentage purifies our remaining wealth and blesses it.

## Who Receives Zakat

The Quran specifies eight categories of recipients: the poor, the needy, Zakat administrators, those whose hearts are to be reconciled, slaves, those in debt, in the path of Allah, and travellers in need.

## Purpose of Zakat

Zakat reduces poverty, cleanses our wealth, and reminds us that all provision is from Allah. It creates a caring society where we support one another.

## Zakat al-Fitr

Zakat al-Fitr is a separate charity we give before Eid al-Fitr. It is obligatory on every one of us who has enough food for our family, and it purifies our fast from any shortcomings.

## Sadaqah: Voluntary Charity

Beyond Zakat, we are encouraged to give voluntary charity whenever possible. Even a smile is charity, and small regular donations bring us great reward.

## Zakat on Knowledge

Our scholars teach that sharing beneficial knowledge is a form of Zakat. Teaching others what we know is a way of giving charity that continues to benefit.

## Common Mistakes

Some among us delay paying Zakat or calculate it incorrectly. Others give Zakat with pride or reminders, which destroys its reward. We should give Zakat secretly and sincerely for Allah's pleasure.
