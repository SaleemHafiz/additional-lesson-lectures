# Cleanliness: Half of Faith

## Importance of Cleanliness

Cleanliness is an essential part of our faith. The Prophet said, "Cleanliness is half of faith," showing how highly we value purity.

## Physical Cleanliness

We are required to keep our bodies, clothes, and surroundings clean. We perform wudu before prayer and bathe regularly to maintain purity.

## Spiritual Cleanliness

Spiritual cleanliness means purifying the heart from sins like jealousy, hatred, arrogance, and dishonesty. True faith requires both outer and inner purity.

## Wudu: Ablution

Wudu is the act of washing specific body parts before prayer. It includes washing the hands, mouth, nose, face, arms, head, and feet.

## Ghusl: Full Bath

Ghusl is a full-body wash required after certain conditions. It ensures complete purification and is an act of worship when done with intention.

## Cleanliness of Clothes and Place

We must pray in clean clothes and on clean ground. Keeping our homes and the mosque clean is also part of our practice.

## Oral Hygiene

The Prophet strongly encouraged using miswak for cleaning teeth. It purifies the mouth and pleases Allah. Toothbrush use is also recommended.

## Cleanliness in Public Spaces

We are taught to keep public spaces clean and not to harm others by littering. We should be a source of cleanliness wherever we go.

## Common Mistakes

Some among us focus on outer cleanliness but ignore purifying their hearts. Others neglect cleanliness in their personal habits without realizing it is part of faith.
