# Dua: The Weapon of the Believer

## Meaning of Dua

Dua is the act of calling upon Allah with humility and sincerity. It is a form of worship that shows our dependence on Allah for everything.

## Importance of Dua

Dua is the essence of worship and a powerful way to connect with Allah. Our Prophet said, "Dua is worship," and encouraged us to make it regularly.

## When to Make Dua

We can make dua at any time, but there are special moments when duas are more likely accepted. These include after prayer, during the last third of the night, while fasting, and between adhan and iqamah.

## Conditions for Accepted Dua

Dua should be made with sincerity, full concentration, and certainty that Allah will respond. Eating halal food, avoiding sin, and having patience also help our duas to be accepted.

## Adab of Making Dua

We should face the qiblah, raise our hands, praise Allah, send blessings on the Prophet, and ask with humility. Duas should be made with hope and fear.

## Dua for Parents

We should make dua for our parents, asking Allah to have mercy on them as they raised us. This is a special act of kindness even after their passing.

## Dua in Times of Difficulty

When facing hardship, we should turn to Allah with sincere dua. The Prophet taught us specific duas for anxiety, sadness, debt, and protection from harm.

## Power of Dua

Dua can change destiny and bring relief from difficulties. Nothing can stop what Allah decrees through dua, and it is a believer's strongest weapon.

## Common Mistakes

Some among us make dua without sincerity or give up because they do not see immediate results. Others ask only in hardship and forget Allah in good times.
