# Faith: The Foundation of Islam

## Meaning of Faith

Faith in our religion is called Iman. It is complete belief in the heart, spoken by the tongue, and shown through actions. Iman increases with obedience and decreases with disobedience.

## Six Pillars of Iman

Iman consists of believing in Allah, His angels, His books, His messengers, the Day of Judgment, and divine decree. These six pillars form the foundation of our faith.

## Faith in Allah

We believe Allah is One, the Creator and Sustainer of all. He alone deserves worship, and nothing happens without His will and knowledge.

## Faith in Angels

Angels are created from light and always obey Allah. They carry out specific tasks like delivering revelation, recording our deeds, and managing natural affairs.

## Faith in Divine Books

Allah revealed books to guide humanity, including the Torah, Zabur, Injil, and Quran. The Quran is the final and most complete revelation, preserved from alteration.

## Faith in Prophets

All prophets were sent by Allah to guide people to Tawheed. We believe in all prophets from Adam to Muhammad without distinguishing between them in faith.

## Faith in the Day of Judgment

Every person will be resurrected and held accountable for their deeds. The righteous will be rewarded with Jannah, and the wicked will face punishment.

## Faith in Divine Decree

Everything happens by Allah's knowledge, will, and decree. Believing in Qadr brings us peace, patience in hardship, and gratitude in ease.

## How Faith Grows

Faith grows through learning about Allah, reading Quran, performing good deeds, and keeping good company. Neglecting worship and sins weaken our faith.
