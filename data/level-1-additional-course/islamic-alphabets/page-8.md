# Greetings: Spreading Peace

## Our Greeting

Our greeting is "Assalamu Alaikum," meaning "Peace be upon you." It is a beautiful dua for peace, mercy, and blessings for the person being greeted.

## Importance of Greetings

Spreading salam is a sunnah that brings love and unity among us. The Prophet said, "You will not enter Paradise until you believe, and you will not believe until you love one another. Spread salam among yourselves."

## The Complete Greeting

The full greeting is "Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuh" meaning "Peace, mercy, and blessings of Allah be upon you." Each additional word increases the reward.

## Responding to Greetings

When someone greets us with salam, we must respond with a better greeting or at least the same. The Quran says in Surah An-Nisa to respond with something better.

## Greeting Non-Muslims

We can greet non-Muslims with salam when there is no hostility. It is a way of showing good character and inviting others to our faith through kindness.

## Greeting at Home

The Prophet taught us to say salam when entering our own homes. This brings blessings to our household and drives away Shaytan.

## Greeting Children

The Prophet would greet children with salam, teaching us to respect and acknowledge them. This builds their confidence and teaches them good manners.

## Other Greetings

Besides salam, we also say "JazakAllahu Khairan," "BarakAllahu Feek," and "MashaAllah" in daily conversations.

## Common Mistakes

Some among us ignore greeting others or only greet those they know. Delaying or not responding to salam is against our manners.
