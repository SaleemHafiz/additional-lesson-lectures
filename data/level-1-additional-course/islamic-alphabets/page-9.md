# Hajj: The Journey of a Lifetime

## Meaning of Hajj

Hajj is the annual pilgrimage to Makkah and the fifth pillar of our faith. It is an obligation for every one of us who is physically and financially able to perform it once in our lifetime.

## When Hajj Occurs

Hajj takes place in the month of Dhul Hijjah, the 12th month of the Islamic calendar. The main rituals occur from the 8th to the 12th of Dhul Hijjah.

## State of Ihram

Before entering Makkah, we enter the state of Ihram by wearing simple white garments and making the intention for Hajj. In Ihram, certain actions are prohibited, like cutting hair and arguing.

## Tawaf: Circling the Kabah

We perform Tawaf by walking around the Kabah seven times in a counterclockwise direction. This shows our devotion and unity as we all worship the same One God.

## Sa'ee: Walking Between Safa and Marwah

We walk seven times between the hills of Safa and Marwah, remembering Hajar's search for water for her son Ismail. This teaches us trust in Allah and perseverance.

## Standing at Arafah

The day at Arafah is the most important part of Hajj. We stand in prayer and supplication from noon to sunset, asking for forgiveness. Our Prophet said, "Hajj is Arafah."

## Muzdalifah and Mina

After Arafah, we spend the night at Muzdalifah collecting pebbles. Then we go to Mina to stone the pillars, symbolizing our rejection of Shaytan as Prophet Ibrahim did.

## Sacrifice and Eid

After stoning, we sacrifice an animal to remember Prophet Ibrahim's obedience to Allah. Men shave or trim their hair, and women cut a small portion. This marks Eid al-Adha.

## Lessons from Hajj

Hajj teaches us equality, humility, patience, and unity. We gather in white garments with no difference between rich and poor, showing our complete submission to Allah.
