# I Am a Muslim: Islam Is My Religion (Condensed)

## Introduction

* “I am Muslim” means living in submission to Allah as a religion and complete way of life.
* Islam is not only identity; it is belief, worship, and obedience based on divine revelation.

## Meaning of Being a Muslim

* A Muslim submits to Allah and follows guidance from the Qur’an and Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
* The word “Muslim” comes from Arabic roots meaning submission, peace, and surrender to God.
* Islam includes belief in the heart and actions in daily life.

## Foundational Belief in Islam

* Islam is built on **Tawheed**: Allah is One without partners.
* The **Shahadah** confirms: no god but Allah, Muhammad ﷺ is His Messenger.
* The Qur’an states Islam is the accepted religion with Allah (Aal-e-Imran 3:19).
* All prophets called to worship One God, including earlier messengers.

## Guidance from Qur’an and Sunnah

* Humans are created to worship Allah and follow His guidance.
* Believers must stay conscious of Allah and avoid false beliefs (3:102).
* The Hadith of Jibreel defines Islam’s five pillars: faith, prayer, charity, fasting, Hajj.
* True faith also includes good character and behavior.

## Responsibilities of a Muslim

* Worship Allah alone through prayer, fasting, and remembrance.
* Follow moral values: honesty, justice, kindness, trustworthiness.
* Avoid oppression (ظلم), injustice, and harm to others.
* Seek knowledge that improves faith and conduct.

## Daily Life Practice

* Islam applies to personal, family, and social life.
* Salah maintains constant connection with Allah.
* Good manners in speech, work, and relations reflect true Islam.
* Islam balances spirituality and worldly life.

## Common Misunderstandings

* Birth in a Muslim family does not equal true practice without belief and action.
* Islam is not only rituals but complete life guidance.
* Some cultural practices are wrongly assumed Islamic.
* True Islam comes from Qur’an and Sunnah, not opinions or traditions.

## Key Takeaways

* Islam is total submission to Allah alone.
* A Muslim is defined by belief, speech, and action.
* Qur’an and Sunnah are the main sources of guidance.
* Being Muslim requires continuous self-improvement and alignment with Islam.
