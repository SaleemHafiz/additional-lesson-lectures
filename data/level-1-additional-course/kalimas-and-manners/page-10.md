# Divine Books in Islam and the Qur’an as the Final Revelation

## Concept of Books of Allah

* In Islam, Allah revealed divine books to guide humanity toward truth and worship of Him alone.
* These books contain commandments, moral guidance, and laws for living a righteous life.
* Revelation (wahy) is given to selected prophets through angel Jibreel (Gabriel).
* Belief in Allah’s revealed books is a fundamental pillar of Islamic faith.
* All divine books originally carried the message of Tawheed, worship of one God.

## Major Revealed Books in Islamic Teachings

* The Qur’an mentions several scriptures revealed before it.
* Tawrah (Torah) was revealed to Prophet Musa (Moses) for the guidance of بني Israel.
* Zabur (Psalms) was revealed to Prophet Dawud (David) as guidance and wisdom.
* Injeel (Gospel) was revealed to Prophet Isa (Jesus) containing guidance and light.
* These books were revealed for specific communities and time periods.

## Purpose of Revealed Books

* Divine books guide humanity in matters of belief, worship, and morality.
* They distinguish truth from falsehood and right from wrong.
* They establish justice and ethical conduct in society.
* They remind people of accountability before Allah on the Day of Judgment.
* They direct humans toward worship of Allah alone without partners.

## Preservation of Earlier Scriptures

* Islamic belief holds that original revelations were pure and true from Allah.
* Over time, some earlier scriptures were altered, lost, or misinterpreted.
* The Quran confirms and corrects what remains true in previous scriptures.
* Because of changes, earlier books are not preserved in their original form today.
* The Quran was revealed as the final and protected message for humanity.

## The Qur’an as the Final Book of Allah

* The Qur’an is the last and final revelation sent by Allah to all mankind.
* It was revealed to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ over approximately 23 years.
* The Qur’an confirms previous scriptures and completes divine guidance.
* It is preserved in its original Arabic text without alteration or corruption.
* Allah Himself guarantees its protection, as stated in Surah Al-Hijr 15:9.

## Structure and Nature of the Qur’an

* The Qur’an is composed of chapters (Surahs) and verses (Ayat).
* It covers beliefs, worship, laws, history, and moral teachings.
* It is both a spiritual guide and a practical code of life.
* Its language is considered unmatched in eloquence and meaning.
* It remains unchanged since its revelation.

## Quranic Role in Guidance

* The Qur’an provides complete guidance for all aspects of life.
* It explains the purpose of human existence and accountability.
* It teaches worship of Allah alone and rejection of shirk.
* It gives moral principles for justice, honesty, and compassion.
* It serves as a lasting source of guidance for all generations.

## Relationship Between Qur’an and Previous Books

* The Qur’an confirms the original messages of earlier prophets.
* It corrects distortions that occurred in earlier scriptures.
* It acts as a final reference for truth in matters of faith.
* It does not reject previous prophets but honors them.
* It unifies all divine messages into a final complete form.

## Importance of Believing in the Qur’an

* Belief in the Qur’an is essential for being a Muslim.
* It affirms trust in Allah’s final guidance.
* It ensures reliance on authentic divine teachings rather than human alteration.
* It strengthens spiritual connection with Allah through recitation and understanding.
* It shapes moral character and daily behavior.

## Common Misunderstandings

* Some believe all previous scriptures are still fully preserved, which is incorrect in Islamic belief.
* Some treat the Qur’an as only a book of rituals rather than complete guidance.
* Some misunderstand translation as equal to the original Arabic Qur’an.
* Some neglect its teachings despite believing in it.
* Correct understanding requires both belief and practice.

## Practical Implementation of Qur’anic Guidance

* A Muslim should recite the Qur’an regularly with understanding.
* A Muslim should apply its teachings in daily life and behavior.
* A Muslim should use it as a standard for truth and morality.
* A Muslim should respect and preserve its teachings in heart and action.
* A Muslim should teach its message to others with clarity and sincerity.

## Key Takeaways

* Allah revealed multiple books to guide humanity throughout history.
* Earlier scriptures were originally true but are not preserved in their original form today.
* The Qur’an is the final, complete, and protected revelation of Allah.
* It confirms, corrects, and completes previous divine messages.
* Belief and practice of the Qur’an are essential for Islamic faith and life.
