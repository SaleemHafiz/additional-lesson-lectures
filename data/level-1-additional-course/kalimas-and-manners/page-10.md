# Divine Books in Islam and the Qur’an as the Final Revelation

## Concept of Divine Books & Major Scriptures

* Allah revealed books to guide humanity to worship Him alone through prophets via Jibreel.
* Major books include Tawrah (Musa), Zabur (Dawud), and Injeel (Isa), each for their nations.

## Purpose of Revelation & Preservation

* Divine books guide belief, worship, morality, justice, and accountability before Allah.
* Earlier scriptures were originally true but later altered, while the Qur’an confirms and corrects them.

## The Qur’an as Final Revelation

* The Qur’an is the final, universal revelation to all humanity through Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
* Allah has guaranteed its preservation, making it unchanged and protected (15:9).

## Nature and Guidance of the Qur’an

* The Qur’an is structured in Surahs and Ayat, covering belief, law, history, and morality.
* It gives complete guidance for life, explaining purpose, worship, and ethical conduct.

## Relationship & Importance of the Qur’an

* The Qur’an confirms original truths of earlier books and unites all divine messages in final form.
* Belief and practice of the Qur’an is essential, requiring recitation, understanding, and application in life.

## Common Misunderstandings & Key Takeaways

* Some wrongly assume earlier scriptures are fully preserved or treat the Qur’an as only ritual text.
* Allah revealed many books, but the Qur’an is the final, complete, and protected guidance for all humanity.
