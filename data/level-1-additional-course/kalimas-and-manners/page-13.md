# Islamic Etiquette of Eating: Cleanliness and Saying Bismillah

## Introduction

* Eating in Islam is not just physical need but an act of worship when done correctly.
* Cleanliness and remembrance of Allah are essential parts of a Muslim lifestyle.

## Importance of Washing Hands Before Eating

* Washing hands removes dirt, germs, and prevents illness.
* Cleanliness before eating reflects Islamic emphasis on purity and health.

## Islamic Wisdom Behind Cleanliness

* Islam promotes cleanliness in both body and behavior as part of faith.
* Clean habits protect health and reflect a believer’s good character.

## Saying Bismillah Before Eating

* Muslims say “Bismillah” before eating to begin in Allah’s name.
* It reminds that food is a blessing and turns eating into worship.

## Hadith Evidence on Bismillah

* The Prophet ﷺ said Shaytan cannot share food when Allah’s name is mentioned (Muslim).
* If forgotten, one should say “Bismillah awwalahu wa akhirahu.”

## Benefits of Saying Bismillah

* It brings barakah (blessing) in food and increases gratitude.
* It builds awareness of Allah and reduces carelessness in daily life.

## Effects of Following These Practices

* Clean hands help prevent disease and improve hygiene.
* Bismillah increases blessings and makes eating spiritually meaningful.

## Common Mistakes in Eating Etiquette

* Some eat without washing hands or forget to say Bismillah.
* Others treat food as only physical need and ignore Sunnah.

## Practical Implementation in Daily Life

* Always wash hands before meals and say Bismillah before eating.
* Teach children these habits and maintain cleanliness regularly.

## Connection Between Hygiene and Faith

* Islam connects physical cleanliness with spiritual discipline.
* Eating with Sunnah turns daily life into worship.

## Key Takeaways

* Cleanliness before eating is essential for health and Sunnah.
* Saying Bismillah brings blessings and spiritual awareness.
* Islam turns simple eating into an act of worship and discipline.
