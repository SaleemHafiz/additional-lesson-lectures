## Meaning of “Allah Made Me”

* “Allah made me” means every human is created directly by Allah with knowledge, power, and purpose, not by chance.
* It affirms Tawheed in Rububiyyah: Allah alone is Creator, Sustainer, and Owner of all existence.

## Qur’anic Foundation of Creation

* Allah created humans in the best form and with deliberate design, and He is Creator of everything.
* Humans and jinn were created only to worship Allah, originating from clay and a single soul.

## Purpose of Creation

* The purpose of life is worship of Allah alone through belief, intention, and action.
* Life is a test of obedience, and humans will be accountable in the Hereafter.

## Recognition of Allah as Creator

* Life, body, intellect, and abilities are all gifts from Allah, not independent possessions.
* This recognition builds humility, gratitude, and awareness of divine wisdom in life events.

## Responsibilities of This Belief

* Humans must use their abilities in ways that please Allah and avoid sin.
* Gratitude is shown through worship, righteous actions, and disciplined moral conduct.

## Common Misunderstandings

* Life is not only material existence; it has a divine purpose and accountability.
* Humans are not fully independent, and creation is not only biological but also divine.

## Practical Impact on Life

* This belief builds patience by treating life as a meaningful test from Allah.
* It reduces arrogance, strengthens ethics, and brings peace through trust in Allah.

## Key Takeaways

* Allah alone is the Creator of everything, and life exists for His worship.
* Life is a test leading to accountability, producing humility and responsibility.

## Final Reminder

* “Allah made me” should guide every belief, action, and decision in daily life.
