# Belief in Angels in Islam: Nature, Purpose, and Understanding

## Introduction

* Belief in angels is part of Islamic faith (Iman) and the unseen world.
* Angels are real creations of Allah, not visible to humans.

## Concept of the Unseen (Al-Ghayb)

* Angels belong to the unseen world created by Allah.
* Belief in the unseen is a sign of true faith (2:3).

## Creation of Angels

* Angels are created from light (Nur) as mentioned in Hadith.
* They do not eat, sleep, or disobey Allah.

## Nature of Angels

* Angels are sinless and always obey Allah.
* They worship Allah and carry out His commands.

## Major Angels

* Jibreel brings revelation, Mikaeel manages provision, Israfeel will blow the trumpet.
* Each angel has a fixed duty assigned by Allah.

## Meaning of Wahi

* Wahi is divine revelation sent by Allah to prophets.
* The Qur’an was revealed through Jibreel to Muhammad ﷺ.

## Purpose of Angels

* They execute Allah’s commands and manage aspects of creation.
* They record deeds and assist believers by Allah’s will.

## Importance of Belief in Angels

* It is one of the six pillars of Iman.
* It strengthens awareness of accountability and the unseen.

## Common Misunderstandings

* Angels are not divine, male/female, or independent beings.
* They only act according to Allah’s command.

## Key Takeaways

* Angels are unseen but real creations of Allah from light.
* They always obey Allah and perform specific duties.
* Belief in them strengthens faith and accountability.
