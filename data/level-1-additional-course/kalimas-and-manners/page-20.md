# Sunnah of Drinking: Islamic Etiquettes, Manners, and Wisdom

## Introduction

* Water is a great blessing from Allah that sustains all life.
* Humans and animals depend on water for survival.
* Islam teaches that even daily actions like drinking can become worship.
* Following the Sunnah in drinking turns an ordinary habit into an act of reward.
* The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught complete manners for eating and drinking.

## Importance of Water as a Blessing

* Water is essential for human survival and health.
* Allah mentions water as a source of life in the Quran.
* It is a shared necessity for all creation, believers and non-believers alike.
* Its availability is a sign of Allah’s mercy.
* Recognizing this blessing leads to gratitude and mindfulness.

## Concept of Sunnah in Daily Life

* Sunnah means the teachings, actions, and approvals of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
* Following Sunnah brings reward (Thawab) from Allah.
* It transforms everyday actions into worship when done with intention.
* It builds discipline and moral character.
* It connects a believer to the lifestyle of the Prophet ﷺ.

## General Manners of Drinking in Islam

* A Muslim should begin drinking with the name of Allah (Bismillah).
* Drinking should be done with awareness and gratitude.
* Excess and waste should be avoided in all situations.
* Cleanliness and respect for food and drink are essential.
* Every action should reflect humility before Allah.

## Drinking with the Right Hand

* The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ encouraged using the right hand for eating and drinking.
* The right hand is associated with honor and cleanliness in Islamic teachings.
* Using the right hand shows respect for Sunnah.
* It distinguishes believers in daily conduct.
* It builds discipline in small but meaningful habits.

## Sitting While Drinking

* Islam teaches to sit while drinking whenever possible.
* Sitting reflects humility and proper manners.
* The Prophet ﷺ discouraged drinking while standing in normal situations.
* Sitting helps avoid haste and carelessness.
* It encourages mindful and calm behavior.

## Avoiding Blowing into Drinks

* Blowing into a drink is discouraged in Islamic etiquette.
* It helps maintain hygiene and cleanliness.
* It prevents discomfort for others sharing the same vessel.
* It reflects respect for food and drink.
* It promotes healthy and considerate habits.

## Drinking in Three Sips

* The Prophet ﷺ used to drink in three separate sips.
* This method is healthier and more disciplined than gulping quickly.
* It allows better appreciation of the blessing of water.
* It prevents discomfort in the body.
* It reflects moderation in all actions.

## Saying Bismillah Before Drinking

* A Muslim should say “Bismillah” before drinking.
* It reminds the believer that the blessing comes from Allah.
* It turns drinking into an act of worship.
* It brings spiritual reward and awareness.
* It protects from forgetfulness and negligence.

## Saying Alhamdulillah After Drinking

* After drinking, a Muslim should say “Alhamdulillah” (All praise belongs to Allah).
* It expresses gratitude for the blessing of water.
* It acknowledges Allah as the Provider of all sustenance.
* It increases blessings in what is consumed.
* It builds a habit of continuous gratitude.

## Du’a After Drinking Milk

* The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught a special supplication after drinking milk.
* The du’a is: “Allahumma barik lana fihi wa zidna minhu.”
* It means: “O Allah, bless it for us and increase it for us.”
* It shows gratitude and request for continued blessing.
* It reflects awareness of Allah’s provision in specific foods.

## Avoiding Waste of Water

* Islam strictly discourages wasting water even during worship.
* Water should be used responsibly and respectfully.
* The Prophet ﷺ advised moderation even when using abundant resources.
* Wasting reflects ingratitude toward Allah’s blessings.
* Conservation is part of Islamic ethics.

## Common Mistakes in Drinking Habits

* Drinking without saying Bismillah is a missed Sunnah.
* Drinking while standing unnecessarily is against preferred etiquette.
* Gulping water quickly without mindfulness is discouraged.
* Blowing into drinks shows lack of proper manners.
* Wasting water goes against Islamic guidance.

## Benefits of Following Sunnah in Drinking

* It brings reward from Allah for simple daily actions.
* It builds discipline and mindfulness in life.
* It strengthens connection with the Prophet ﷺ.
* It improves physical and spiritual well-being.
* It encourages gratitude and humility.

## Key Takeaways

* Drinking water is a blessing from Allah and should be done with gratitude.
* Following Sunnah transforms drinking into worship.
* Proper manners include sitting, using the right hand, and avoiding waste.
* Saying Bismillah before and Alhamdulillah after is essential practice.
* Islam teaches balance between physical needs and spiritual awareness.
