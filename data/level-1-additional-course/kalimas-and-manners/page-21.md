# Sunnah of Drinking: Islamic Etiquettes, Manners, and Wisdom

## Introduction

* Water is a major blessing from Allah and essential for life.
* Drinking can become worship when done with Sunnah.

## Importance of Water

* Water is a source of life and mercy from Allah.
* It reminds us to be grateful.

## Sunnah Concept

* Sunnah is the Prophet ﷺ’s way in daily actions.
* Following it brings reward and discipline.

## Basic Manners

* Say Bismillah before and Alhamdulillah after drinking.
* Avoid waste and keep cleanliness.

## Right Hand

* Drink with the right hand as Sunnah.
* It shows respect and good manners.

## Sitting

* Sit while drinking when possible.
* It shows humility and calmness.

## No Blowing

* Do not blow into drinks.
* It keeps hygiene and respect.

## Three Sips

* Drink in three sips, not all at once.
* It teaches moderation.

## Milk Du’a

* Say: “Allahumma barik lana fihi wa zidna minhu.”
* It asks blessing from Allah.

## Avoid Waste

* Do not waste water in any case.
* Water is a trust from Allah.

## Mistakes

* Ignoring Sunnah or wasting water.
* Drinking carelessly without remembrance.

## Benefits

* Brings reward and gratitude.
* Builds discipline and awareness.

## Key Takeaways

* Drinking becomes worship with Sunnah.
* Use Bismillah, right hand, and moderation.
* Islam teaches gratitude in every sip.
