# Manners of Talking in Islam

## Introduction

* Speech is one of the greatest blessings given by Allah to human beings.
* Islam teaches that how we speak reflects our faith and character.
* The tongue can be a source of great good or great harm depending on its use.
* Good manners in speech are part of Islamic ethics and daily conduct.

## The Power of the Tongue

* The tongue can spread truth, kindness, and guidance when used correctly.
* It can also cause harm through lies, insults, and harmful speech.
* Islamic teachings highlight that speech has real consequences in this life and the Hereafter.
* A person’s words often reveal the condition of their heart.

## Story Lesson: The Tongue as Good and Bad

* A wise minister explained to a king that the tongue is both the best and worst part of the body.
* When used for good speech, it spreads goodness and benefits others.
* When used for bad speech, it spreads harm and evil among people.
* This example teaches that responsibility lies in how a person uses their words.

## Qur’anic Guidance on Speech

* Allah commands believers to speak kindly and respectfully to others.
* The Qur’an teaches that good speech is part of righteous behavior.
* Speaking good words helps build peace, understanding, and unity in society.
* Harmful speech is discouraged because it damages relationships and hearts.

## Example of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

* The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was the best example of good manners in speech.
* His words were always wise, clear, and beneficial to others.
* People enjoyed speaking with him because he spoke with kindness and truth.
* He always avoided harsh or unnecessary speech.

## Style of the Prophet’s Speech

* Hazrat Aisha (RA) described that the Prophet ﷺ spoke clearly and slowly.
* He did not speak quickly or in a confusing manner.
* He spoke in a way that people could easily understand and reflect on.
* His speech was calm, meaningful, and respectful.

## Importance of Good Speech

* Good speech strengthens relationships between people.
* It spreads peace, love, and understanding in society.
* It protects a person from sin and harmful consequences.
* It reflects strong faith and good character.

## Common Mistakes in Speech

* Speaking without thinking can lead to harm and regret.
* Using harsh or insulting words damages relationships.
* Spreading lies or gossip is strongly discouraged in Islam.
* Interrupting others or speaking rudely shows poor manners.

## Practical Manners of Talking

* Always think before speaking to ensure words are beneficial.
* Speak kindly and respectfully to everyone.
* Avoid lies, gossip, and harmful speech.
* Listen carefully and speak clearly when necessary.

## Key Takeaways

* The tongue can be a source of both good and harm.
* Islam strongly emphasizes speaking good and beneficial words.
* The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is the best example of excellent speech.
* Good manners in speech improve both personal character and society.

## Final Reminder

* Every Muslim should guard their tongue and use it wisely.
* Speaking good words is a form of worship when done sincerely for Allah.
* A believer should always aim to make their speech a source of benefit, not harm.
