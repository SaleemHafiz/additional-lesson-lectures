# Manners of Talking in Islam

## Introduction

* Speech is a blessing from Allah.
* It reflects a person’s faith and character.
* Words can bring good or harm.

## Power of the Tongue

* The tongue can spread truth or lies.
* It affects relationships and hearts.
* Speech has consequences in life and Hereafter.

## Lesson Example

* The tongue can be best or worst.
* Good speech benefits others.
* Bad speech causes harm and conflict.

## Qur’anic Guidance

* Islam commands kind and good speech.
* Good words build peace and unity.
* Harmful speech is discouraged.

## Example of Prophet ﷺ

* The Prophet ﷺ always spoke kindly.
* His speech was clear and wise.
* He avoided harsh or useless talk.

## His Style of Speech

* He spoke slowly and clearly.
* People understood him easily.
* His words were calm and meaningful.

## Importance

* Good speech builds strong relationships.
* It spreads peace and respect.
* It shows good faith and character.

## Common Mistakes

* Speaking without thinking.
* Using rude or insulting words.
* Spreading lies or gossip.

## Practical Manners

* Think before speaking.
* Speak kindly and respectfully.
* Avoid lies and harmful talk.

## Key Takeaways

* Speech can build or destroy.
* Islam teaches careful speech.
* The Prophet ﷺ is the best example.

## Final Reminder

* Guard your tongue daily.
* Let your words bring benefit, not harm.
