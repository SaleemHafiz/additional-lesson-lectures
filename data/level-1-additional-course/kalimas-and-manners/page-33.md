# Exercise Corner: Sunnah Manners of Talking

## Introduction

* Speech is a blessing from Allah used to communicate and build relations.
* Islam teaches speaking with manners as the Prophet ﷺ showed.

## Meaning and Importance of Talking Manners

* Speech is a gift from Allah and must be used responsibly.
* Good speech is part of a Muslim’s faith and character, guided by Sunnah.

## Key Ideas from the Exercise (Matching Concepts)

* Speech should always be used in a good and beneficial way.
* We must listen calmly, avoid interrupting, and speak kindly as taught by the Prophet ﷺ.

## Sunnah Manners of Talking

* Begin with peace, speak calmly and clearly, and avoid rushing.
* Listen properly, respect elders, be kind to younger ones, and avoid anger or shouting.

## Respectful Communication in Islam

* Respectful speech means valuing others’ feelings and status.
* The Prophet ﷺ spoke with clarity, kindness, and wisdom in all situations.

## Benefits of Following Sunnah Speech

* Sunnah speech brings love, respect, and peace in society.
* Allah is pleased when speech is truthful, kind, and beneficial.

## Answers to Study Questions

* The Prophet ﷺ spoke wisely and kindly in all situations.
