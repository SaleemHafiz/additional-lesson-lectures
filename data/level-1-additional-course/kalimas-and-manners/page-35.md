# Cleanliness and Manners in Daily Life (Brushing, Hygiene, Eating and Drinking)

## Introduction

* Islam links daily habits with faith, turning routine actions into worship.
* Cleanliness is essential for health, discipline, and spiritual purity, as taught by the Prophet ﷺ.

## Importance of Oral Cleanliness

* Islam encourages keeping the mouth clean for hygiene and good manners.
* The Prophet ﷺ recommended miswak, and toothbrush use is also allowed for the same purpose.

## General Cleanliness in Islam

* Cleanliness reflects a Muslim’s identity and discipline in life.
* This includes rinsing mouth, trimming nails, bathing, combing hair, and wearing clean clothes.

## Importance of Clean Living

* Clean habits protect health and improve social confidence.
* Islam teaches both outer cleanliness and inner purity of intention.

## Eating and Drinking in Islam

* Food and drink are blessings from Allah and must be halal.
* Eating halal with right intention is obedience and brings reward.

## Sunnah Etiquettes of Eating

* Eat in moderation, preferably when truly hungry.
* Wash hands before eating and follow Sunnah manners for blessings.

## Wisdom Behind Cleanliness and Eating Manners

* Cleanliness protects body and society from harm.
* Sunnah habits build discipline, gratitude, and self-control.

## Common Mistakes

* Ignoring hygiene causes health and social problems.
* Overeating and careless manners reduce discipline and gratitude.

## Key Takeaways

* Cleanliness is a core part of Islamic daily life.
* Eating and hygiene become worship when done with Sunnah manners.

## Final Reminder

* Cleanliness is a daily duty, not occasional practice.
* Small clean habits bring both health and Allah’s reward.
