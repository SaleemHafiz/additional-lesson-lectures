# Cleanliness and Eating Manners in Islam

## Introduction

* Islam teaches that cleanliness, eating, and hygiene are part of a believer’s character and worship.
* The Prophet ﷺ gave practical guidance for daily cleanliness and good manners.

## Importance of Oral Hygiene

* Islam encourages regular cleaning of teeth as part of cleanliness.
* The Prophet ﷺ recommended miswak (Bukhari, Muslim), and toothbrush use is also allowed.

## General Cleanliness in Daily Life

* Cleanliness includes mouth rinsing, nail trimming, bathing, hair care, and clean clothing.
* These habits protect health and reflect discipline and respect.

## Cleanliness as a Way of Life

* Clean habits prevent illness and improve social confidence.
* Islam teaches both outer cleanliness and inner purity of intention.

## Eating and Drinking in Islam

* Food and drink are blessings from Allah and must be halal.
* Eating halal with right intention is obedience and brings reward.

## Etiquettes of Eating in Islam

* Eat in moderation and only when truly hungry.
* Wash hands before eating and follow Sunnah manners.

## Wisdom Behind Cleanliness and Eating Manners

* Cleanliness protects health and prevents disease.
* Sunnah habits build discipline, gratitude, and self-control.

## Common Mistakes

* Neglecting hygiene harms health and social respect.
* Overeating and careless manners reduce discipline and gratitude.

## Key Takeaways

* Cleanliness is essential in Islam and daily life.
* Eating halal with good manners is a form of worship.

## Final Reminder

* Cleanliness and manners should be daily habits, not occasional actions.
* Small Sunnah actions bring health, discipline, and Allah’s reward.
