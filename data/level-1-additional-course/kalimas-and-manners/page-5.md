# Allah as Al-Razzaq (The Provider)

## Meaning and Concept

* Allah is **Al-Razzaq**, the One who continuously provides sustenance to all creation in every form of life.
* Provision includes food, health, knowledge, opportunities, and all means of survival, and it comes only by Allah’s will.

## Qur’anic Guidance on Sustenance

* The Qur’an states that Allah is the Provider, guarantees every creature’s sustenance, and gives provision without measure (51:58, 11:6, 3:27).
* It also teaches that all blessings come from Allah and He alone controls provision (16:53, 29:60).

## What Allah Provides

* Allah provides all physical needs like air, water, food, and natural resources, along with abilities like sight, hearing, and intellect.
* He also provides emotional, social, and economic means through nature and opportunities for earning.

## Importance of Recognizing Allah as Provider

* This belief strengthens faith, trust, and reliance on Allah while reducing anxiety about sustenance.
* It builds humility and encourages effort without greed, injustice, or overdependence on worldly means.

## Gratitude and Responsibility

* Gratitude increases blessings and is shown through worship, obedience, and using blessings correctly (14:7).
* It also includes charity, kindness, and maintaining humility instead of arrogance.

## Common Mistakes

* Some wrongly believe success is only due to personal effort and ignore Allah’s will.
* Others misunderstand reliance on Allah as leaving effort or use unlawful means for provision.

## Practical Application

* A believer trusts Allah in provision while still working hard and responsibly in halal ways.
* They give charity, stay grateful, and help others as part of sharing Allah’s blessings.

## Key Lessons

* Allah alone is Al-Razzaq, the true Provider of all creation.
* Provision is a test that requires gratitude, effort, and reliance on Allah.
