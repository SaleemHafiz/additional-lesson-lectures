# Allah as the Giver of Food and Sustenance

## Concept of Divine Provision in Food

* Allah is the true provider of all food and nourishment for every living being.
* Food is a blessing from Allah, while human effort only works by His permission.

## Qur’anic Foundation of Food Provision

* Allah produces crops, fruits, and sustenance from the earth as mercy for humans.
* Rain, soil growth, and food supply are signs of His control and power (Abasa 80:24–32).

## Examples of Allah’s Food Blessings

* Allah provides fruits, grains, milk, dates, and many foods for human benefit.
* These foods are designed with perfect nutrition, taste, and survival value.

## Hadith and Islamic Teachings on Food

* The Prophet ﷺ taught thanking Allah after eating and praising Him for provision.
* He also taught moderation, simplicity, and avoiding food waste.

## Purpose and Wisdom Behind Food Provision

* Food keeps humans alive so they can worship Allah and fulfill duties.
* It also tests gratitude, awareness, and dependence on Allah.

## Attitude of Gratitude in Eating

* Muslims begin food with Allah’s name and end with praise and thanks.
* Sharing food and avoiding waste are signs of true gratitude.

## Common Mistakes in Understanding Food Provision

* Some think food is only due to human effort and forget Allah.
* Others waste food or eat without gratitude and awareness.

## Practical Lessons for Daily Life

* A believer eats with gratitude, halal choice, and awareness of Allah.
* Food should be respected, shared, and never wasted.

## Key Takeaways

* All food comes from Allah, even through human effort.
* Food is a blessing and a responsibility.
* Recognizing Allah as provider builds faith, humility, and discipline.
