# Belief in Allah: The One Creator and Sustainer

## Concept of Tawheed (Oneness of Allah)

* Islam teaches that Allah is the only Creator and Lord of all existence.
* Tawheed means believing that Allah is One without any partners or equals.
* Allah has no beginning and no end and is independent of all creation.
* Everything in the universe depends on Him, while He depends on nothing.
* Belief in Allah’s oneness is the foundation of Islamic faith.

## Quranic Foundation of Allah’s Oneness

* The Quran clearly states that Allah is One in Surah Al-Ikhlas 112:1.
* Allah is described as Al-Samad, meaning the One upon whom all depend.
* The Quran rejects any idea of partners, family, or offspring for Allah.
* Allah declares that nothing is equal or comparable to Him in Surah Ash-Shura 42:11.
* The Quran emphasizes that Allah alone deserves worship and obedience.

## Attributes of Allah in Islamic Belief

* Allah has no father, mother, sons, or daughters, as He is beyond creation.
* Allah is eternal and not dependent on time, space, or physical form.
* Allah is unseen by human eyes in this world, but He sees everything.
* Allah has complete knowledge of all actions, thoughts, and intentions.
* Allah is the Most Powerful and has full control over the universe.

## Relationship Between Allah and Creation

* All human beings are created by Allah and are dependent on Him for survival.
* Every breath, heartbeat, and movement exists by Allah’s permission.
* No creation shares divine qualities or authority with Allah.
* Allah is closer in knowledge to humans than they are to themselves.
* Creation exists to recognize, obey, and worship Allah alone.

## Quranic Evidence of Dependence on Allah

* The Quran states that Allah is free of need while all creation is in need of Him in Surah Fatir 35:15.
* Allah alone gives life and causes death according to His will.
* Allah provides guidance, sustenance, and mercy to all beings.
* The Quran repeatedly warns against associating partners with Allah.
* Worship is exclusively for Allah because only He deserves it.

## Allah’s Knowledge and Awareness

* Allah sees all actions whether public or hidden.
* Nothing in the heavens or earth is hidden from Allah.
* Allah knows what is in the hearts and intentions of people.
* Human perception is limited, but Allah’s knowledge is complete.
* This belief encourages accountability and moral responsibility.

## Love and Mercy of Allah

* Allah’s mercy is greater than His anger as mentioned in authentic hadith.
* Allah provides guidance to protect humans from harm and misguidance.
* Every blessing in life is a sign of Allah’s love and care.
* Allah gives opportunities for repentance and forgiveness.
* Allah’s love is expressed through guidance, protection, and provision.

## Common Misunderstandings

* Some people incorrectly believe Allah has family or physical form.
* Some assume Allah is limited like human beings, which contradicts Islamic belief.
* Some think Allah is distant, while Islam teaches He is fully aware of all creation.
* Some forget that dependence on Allah is constant and absolute.
* Some misunderstand unseen nature of Allah as absence, which is incorrect.

## Practical Understanding in Daily Life

* A believer should worship Allah alone without associating partners.
* A believer should trust Allah in all situations and decisions.
* A believer should be mindful that Allah sees every action.
* A believer should avoid sin because nothing is hidden from Allah.
* A believer should develop gratitude for being guided to know Allah.

## Key Lessons

* Allah is One, unique, and without any partner or equal.
* All creation depends entirely on Allah, while He depends on none.
* Allah is unseen but fully aware of everything in existence.
* Worship and obedience belong only to Allah alone.
* True faith requires understanding, acceptance, and practice of Tawheed.
