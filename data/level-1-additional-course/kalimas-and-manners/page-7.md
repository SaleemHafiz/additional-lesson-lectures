# Belief in Allah: The One Creator and Sustainer

## Concept of Tawheed (Oneness of Allah)

* Allah is the only Creator and Lord of all existence.
* Tawheed means Allah is One, without partner, equal, or helper, and all creation depends on Him.

## Qur’anic Foundation of Allah’s Oneness

* The Qur’an declares Allah is One and uniquely self-sufficient (112:1, 112:2).
* It rejects any partner, family, or likeness for Allah and affirms His absolute uniqueness (42:11).

## Attributes of Allah in Islamic Belief

* Allah has no parents, children, or physical form and is beyond creation.
* He is eternal, unseen, all-knowing, and fully in control of everything.

## Relationship Between Allah and Creation

* All creation depends completely on Allah for life, movement, and survival.
* Nothing shares His divine qualities, and humans exist to worship Him alone.

## Qur’anic Evidence of Dependence on Allah

* Allah is free of need, while all creation is dependent on Him (35:15).
* He alone gives life, death, sustenance, guidance, and mercy.

## Allah’s Knowledge and Awareness

* Allah sees everything, hidden or open, and knows what is in hearts and minds.
* This complete awareness builds accountability and moral responsibility.

## Love and Mercy of Allah

* Allah’s mercy is greater than His anger and includes guidance and forgiveness.
* Every blessing and opportunity to repent is a sign of His care and love.

## Common Misunderstandings

* Some wrongly think Allah has form, family, or human-like limits.
* Others assume Allah is distant, while He is fully aware of every detail of creation.

## Practical Understanding in Daily Life

* A believer worships Allah alone and avoids all forms of shirk.
* Awareness of Allah’s knowledge encourages honesty, obedience, and gratitude.

## Key Lessons

* Allah is One, unique, and absolutely independent.
* All creation depends on Him, while He depends on none.
* True faith requires belief in Tawheed, awareness, and action.
