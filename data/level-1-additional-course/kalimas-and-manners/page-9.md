*First Kalima (Tayyab)*
لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ اللهِ
La ilaha illallah, Muhammadur Rasulullah
Translation: There is no God but Allah, Muhammad is the messenger of Allah

*Second Kalima (Shahadat)*
أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيْكَ لَهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُوْلُهُ
Ash-hadu an la ilaha illallah, wahdahu la sharika lah, wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluh
Translation: I bear witness that there is no God but Allah, the One, there is no partner with Him, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and messenger

*Third Kalima (Tamjeed)*
سُبْحَانَ اللهِ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ وَلَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَاللهُ أَكْبَرُ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللهِ الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيْمِ
Subhanallah, walhamdulillah, wa la ilaha illallah, wallahu akbar, wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billahil aliyil azeem
Translation: Glory be to Allah, all praise be to Allah, there is no God but Allah, Allah is the Greatest, there is no power nor strength except with Allah, the Most High, the Most Great
